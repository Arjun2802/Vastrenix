1. Three Ways to Trigger Account Deletion

Vastrenix provides three access points for full account and data removal:

1. In the Web App

Go to More → Account → Delete account. Opens an in-app confirmation modal, re-authenticates password, requires typing DELETE, and immediately invokes the deletion API.

2. Public Website (Here)

Use the standalone session-less form below. Meets Google Play requirements for users to permanently remove their account and data without reinstalling the native app.

3. By Email

Send a request to contact@vastrenix.com from your registered email address. Processed within 30 days per statutory privacy policy.

Android App Users: Tapping "Delete account" in the native Android app automatically opens this verified portal (https://vastrenix.com/delete-account/) via an origin-locked native bridge message.

2. Delete Account Confirmation

⚠️ This cannot be undone:
  • For Store Owners: Your login and your whole store — items, stock, bills, customers, khata, dealers, staff access, and settings — will be deleted for everyone. Remaining paid plan time is not refunded.
  • For Staff Members: Your login will be deleted and you will lose access to this store. The employer store's data stays with the owner.
Or request deletion via email: contact@vastrenix.com

3. Summary: What is Kept vs. Deleted

Per statutory data destruction policies and Indian commercial law:

Data Type Action Upon Deletion Reason
Firebase Auth Login Permanently Deleted User cannot sign in again.
User Profile & Mapping Permanently Deleted Removed from /users/{uid}.
Support Tickets Permanently Deleted Cleared from /supportRequests.
Store Data (Owner) Permanently Deleted Full recursive deletion of products, variants, bills, Khata, staff, etc.
Store Code Permanently Deleted Removed from /codes/{code} so code cannot be reused.
Staff Record (Staff) Permanently Deleted Access revoked and removed from store's staff list.
Plan Payment Records Anonymized & Kept Email and store name erased; financial record kept for GST / income tax law.
Audit Log Entry Appended Timestamped record that deletion occurred (with email redacted).
